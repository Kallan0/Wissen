# v1.25.0 - Deterministic Hallucination Guard

Date: 2026-10-28

## Summary

Changed resume generation from persuasive completion to evidence-only editing and added deterministic validation after every Ollama or Groq response.

## Prompt contract

- Treat the job description only as employer requirements, never as candidate evidence.
- Require exact support for organization, internship title, project name, institution, degree, date, location, technology, certification, link, and numeric metric.
- Require empty arrays or strings when evidence is missing.
- Prohibit converting skills, coursework, or job requirements into employment or projects.
- Prohibit degree expansion and guessed campus, graduation date, GPA, coursework, or honors.
- Limit bullets to supported facts without filler.

## Deterministic validation

- Overwrite identity and contact values with verified user/profile values when available.
- Remove an experience unless both company and role appear in verified context.
- Remove a project unless its name appears in verified context.
- Remove education unless both institution and degree appear in verified context.
- Remove unsupported skills, technologies, links, dates, locations, bullet claims, and numeric metrics.
- Attach removal counts under `_grounding_validation` for auditability.
- Remove fictional records and hardcoded skill lists from the offline fallback path.
- Include hybrid-retrieved raw PDF/text excerpts in the actual generation prompt.

## Verification

- Added adversarial coverage for invented employers, internships, projects, colleges, degrees, skills, contacts, and metrics.
- Grounding, provider, retrieval, and E2E suite: 59 tests passed with one third-party deprecation warning.
