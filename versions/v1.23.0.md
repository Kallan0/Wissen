# v1.23.0 - Redundancy-Safe Ingestion and PDF Content

Date: 2026-10-28

## Summary

Added deterministic duplicate prevention and concise resume-content constraints from ingestion through PDF export.

## Changes

- Made ChromaDB writes idempotent with canonical content fingerprints and stable IDs.
- Remove historical exact duplicate vectors whenever their collection receives new data.
- Merge duplicate questionnaire experiences, projects, skills, bullets, and technologies before ingestion.
- Reconstruct overview and detail chunks as one logical experience or project instead of exposing every vector as a separate resume record.
- Preserve structured experience bullets and project highlights in Chroma metadata.
- Require the AI to include each internship and project once, avoid repeated achievements, and use 2-4 distinct bullets of no more than 30 words.
- Consolidate duplicate model output again at PDF export as a deterministic final safeguard.
- Move project descriptions into bullet lists, cap experience entries at four bullets and projects at three, and remove repeated bullets across sections.

## Verification

- Added unit coverage for repeated profile entities, vector re-ingestion, historical duplicate cleanup, and PDF content consolidation.
- Full backend suite: 85 tests passed (one third-party deprecation warning).
- Confirmed the isolated E2E suite cannot resolve to the application ChromaDB path.
